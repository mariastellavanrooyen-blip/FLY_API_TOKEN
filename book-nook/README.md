# 📚 Book Nook

A reading tracker styled like a watercolour commonplace book, with coloured index tabs down the side.

## How to open it
Double-click `book-nook/index.html` (or drag it into Chrome, Edge or Safari). You don't need to install anything.
To use it like an app, bookmark it. On a phone, choose **Add to Home Screen**.

## What's inside (the tabs)
1. **Reading Now**: books you're reading, each with a page-progress bar and a **▶ Start reading** timer
2. **Books Read**: finished books, with star ratings and the date you finished
3. **Wish List**: books you want to read. Mark one as *Must read!* or move it to Reading Now
4. **Progress Sheet**: your yearly book goal, daily and weekly reading-time goals, your streak, pages read, and charts
5. **Reading Log**: every reading session (date, time, pages, note)
6. **Quotes**: favourite lines collected from all your books
7. **Settings & Backup**: edit goals, download or restore a backup, erase everything

## Adding books
- **Find it online**: type a title, author or ISBN and press *Search*. The details, cover and page count come
  from the free [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org) catalogue. This needs internet.
- **Upload photo** / **Take photo**: add your own picture of the cover. On a phone, *Take photo* opens the camera.
- **Attach files**: add e-books, PDFs, notes or receipts to any book.

## Your data
Everything is saved in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Use **Download backup** now and then. To move to another computer or phone, use **Restore backup** there.
