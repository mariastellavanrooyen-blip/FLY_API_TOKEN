# 🌼 Abide

A Bible companion with a cottage-core look: linen paper, a moss-green side panel, washi-tape labels and
sticky notes. It is built to help you grow closer to God, one small step at a time. "Grow deep, not fast."

## Get it as an app (computer or phone)
Abide is an installable web app with its own icon and window, and it works without internet once opened.
It is published with GitHub Pages at:

**https://mariastellavanrooyen-blip.github.io/FLY_API_TOKEN/abide/**

To put the latest version online, merge this branch into the repo's default branch. Then on GitHub go to
**Actions → "Deploy frontend + Book Nook + Tend + Abide to GitHub Pages" → Run workflow**.

- **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android:** browser menu (⋮) → **Install app**
- **Chrome or Edge on a computer:** the install icon at the right end of the address bar

You can also just double-click `index.html` to use it without installing.

## What's inside
1. **Home**: verse for today, your daily rhythm (Pray, Read, Reflect, Pray) with a streak, and small widgets for
   Proverbs 31, your fast, today's Bible reading and the next lesson.
2. **Proverbs 31 Woman**: ten marks of a godly woman, each with four daily habits to tick.
   - *Progress sheet*: a weekly grid (traits × days), a focus area for the week, and an 8-week trend.
   - *Monthly growth*: rate each area 1–5 every month and see how it changed since last month.
   - *My notes* for each area, and *In today's world, she…*.
3. **Fasting & Prayer**: choose a kind of fast (a meal, sunrise to sunset, water, Daniel, media, your own), why
   you're fasting and who you're praying for. A live timer, "when hunger comes" prayer prompts, a fasting journal,
   prayers for before/during/after, planned fasts, your fast history, and teaching on biblical fasting
   (including when to check with a doctor first).
4. **Bible Study**: a 6-week plan (Knowing God, Identity, Trusting God, Hearing His voice, Purpose & calling,
   Becoming like Jesus) with notes for every day and a question each week.
5. **Scripture**: 146 verses (KJV) in 18 topics with search, saved verses, a memorise helper that hides words step by step,
   your own verses as sticky notes, and **Renew my mind** (write the lie, then the truth God says).
6. **Walk With God**: 22 short lessons (grace, prayer, Bible study, renewing your mind, fasting, the fruit of the Spirit,
   words, forgiveness, stewardship, rest, serving, the armour of God, gratitude, contentment, worry, humility, worship,
   building your home, hearing and obeying God, sharing your faith, honouring God with your body, hope in hard seasons), each with steps to live it and a reflection.
7. **Prayer Journal**: prayer list, answered prayers, and "God moments".
8. **Settings & Backup**: your name, download/restore a backup, erase, and install help.

## Your privacy
Everything is saved automatically in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Each phone or computer keeps
its own copy, so use **Download backup** and **Restore backup** to move it.
